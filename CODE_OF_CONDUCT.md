# Code of Conduct

MintCare follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Please read it before you take part in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, email the maintainer at matthewallwood@protonmail.com. Say what happened, when and where, and who was involved. The maintainer reads every report and handles it under the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
